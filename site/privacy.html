Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Nuzzlebook is a care notebook for parents and caregivers. This policy explains what the app stores, when it uses Apple iCloud, and the choices you have. Nuzzlebook is not intended for children to use on their own and is not medical advice.

What you enter

Depending on the features you use, Nuzzlebook can store:

  • Child profile details such as a name, birth date, optional profile photo, notebook color, and tracking choices.
  • Care notes such as bottle feeds, breastfeeding sessions, diapers, sleep, medicine, timestamps, amounts, and free-form notes.
  • The caregiver name you choose to show beside a note and caregiver shift details.

Where data is stored

Nuzzlebook stores notebook data locally on your iPhone using Apple’s SwiftData storage. The app can be used offline. iCloud is optional: when you enable it, Nuzzlebook uses Apple CloudKit to sync your notebooks and to support Apple’s private sharing sheet.

Each child has a separate notebook route. When you invite another parent or caregiver, everyone you invite can access and edit the records in that child’s shared notebook. A child’s tracking choices travel with that notebook. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy also apply to data processed by iCloud.

Reminders and widgets

Care reminders are optional local notifications. Your chosen intervals and reminder settings are stored on the device; Nuzzlebook uses care timestamps to schedule them. Notification text can include a child’s name and care details, so choose the lock-screen preview setting you prefer in iPhone Settings.

The Home Screen widget shares a local care summary and pending entries with the app through Apple’s app-group storage. This allows the widget and app to use the same notebook. Adding the widget does not send this data to a Nuzzlebook-operated server.

Photos

Photos are optional. You choose a photo through Apple’s Photos picker; Nuzzlebook does not request whole-library access. The selected portrait is resized and newly encoded on the device before it is stored. The encoded portrait is limited in size and does not carry the original camera or location metadata. If iCloud sharing is enabled for that child, the saved portrait follows that notebook’s sharing route.

What we do not do

Nuzzlebook does not use third-party advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. The app does not create a separate Nuzzlebook account. iCloud uses the Apple account already signed in on the device for Apple’s storage and sharing services.

Deletion and retention

Your local notebook remains on your device until you delete its records, delete the child from Settings, or remove the app. Deleting a child removes that child’s profile, care events, and shifts from the local notebook. If the notebook is shared and iCloud is available, the deletion is sent through the shared notebook so participants receive it. Caregiver attribution identities can remain when another child’s notebook still uses them.

Apple controls retention and deletion in iCloud according to its services and your account settings. Nuzzlebook does not keep a separate copy on a Nuzzlebook-operated server.

Requests and support

For privacy questions or help with your notebook, email support@tillnext.app or visit Nuzzlebook Support. Include only the information needed to explain your request; you do not need to send your child’s care history. We use information you send to respond to your request.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not use advertising, analytics scripts, or cookies added by Nuzzlebook. GitHub may process technical request information to serve and protect the site. GitHub’s privacy statement applies to its hosting and support services.

Changes to this policy

If Nuzzlebook’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. The version of the policy that applies is the one published when you use the relevant feature.
