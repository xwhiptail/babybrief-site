BabyBrief Support

BabyBrief is being prepared for the App Store. If you are trying an early build, you can report a problem or ask a question below.

Share a notebook

Choose your child, open Settings, and tap Enable iCloud. Then use Invite caregiver. Each person needs BabyBrief and their own iCloud account. Invite caregivers separately for each child you want to share.

Check a missing update

Make sure both parents have selected the same child. Check the iCloud status in Settings and use Sync now. If iCloud is unavailable, your changes remain on your phone until they can sync.

Change a profile or tracking choice

Use Settings to update a child’s name, photo, theme, or tracking choices. Turning a care category off keeps its earlier entries in History.

Delete a child

Choose the child, then open Settings → Delete child. Read the confirmation carefully: it removes the child’s notebook and care records, including from the shared notebook when iCloud sync is available.

Ask for help

Email us with what you expected, what happened, your iOS version, and the BabyBrief version. Include only the information needed to explain the problem; you do not need to send your child’s care history.
