A little help

Nuzzlebook Support

Nuzzlebook is being prepared for the App Store. If you are trying an early build, you can report a problem or ask a question below.

Share a notebook

Choose your child, open Settings, and tap Enable iCloud. Then use Invite caregiver. Each person needs Nuzzlebook and their own iCloud account. Invite caregivers separately for each child you want to share.

Check a missing update

Make sure both parents have selected the same child. Check the iCloud status in Settings and use Sync now. If iCloud is unavailable, your changes remain on your phone until they can sync.

Change a profile or tracking choice

Use Settings to update a child’s name, photo, theme, or tracking choices. Turning a care category off keeps its earlier entries in History.

Set a reminder

Choose your child, then open Settings → Reminders. Pick a care type, turn its reminder on, set the interval you want and save. Allow notifications when your iPhone asks. A saved care entry updates the next reminder for that care type. Reminders are set on each caregiver’s device.

Tap a reminder to open Nuzzlebook on that child’s Now page. If alerts are missing, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Nuzzlebook, as well as Focus and notification delivery settings.

Record medicine

Tap Medicine on Now. Enter a name, amount and unit, or choose a recent name. Choosing a name does not choose a dose: enter the amount or explicitly reuse a previous dose before saving. Follow your healthcare professional’s instructions.

Add the Home Screen widget

Touch and hold your iPhone’s Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, then search for Nuzzlebook. Set up a child in the app first. Use the widget’s care controls to prepare and save an entry; medicine shortcuts need a previously logged medicine and dose.

Delete a child

Choose the child, then open Settings → Delete child. Read the confirmation carefully: it removes the child’s notebook and care records, including from the shared notebook when iCloud sync is available.

Ask for help

Email us with what you expected, what happened, your iOS version, and the Nuzzlebook version. Include only the information needed to explain the problem; you do not need to send your child’s care history.
